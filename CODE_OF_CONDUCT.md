# Community Conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss technical choices with evidence and
focus feedback on the work, not the person. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
and disclosure of someone else's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation to protect the
community and will consider context and proportionality. Do not publish private
reports or identifying details in issues. Serious conduct violations can be
reported through [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines).

This policy applies to the organization's repositories, discussions and other
official collaboration spaces.
